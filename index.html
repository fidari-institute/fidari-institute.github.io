Research Pillars
Mission

Why Agentic Studies?

As AI systems become increasingly autonomous, we need to know why they do what they do, helping us anticipate their actions. Fidari's main goal is not to prevent certain outcomes, though, but rather to study AI as an organism to discover and investigate its behaviors.


View the Agentic Studies Whitepaper

What does Fidari do?

Fidari is an interdisciplinary community dedicated to implementing Agentic Studies from different perspectives, from psychology to political science. We believe that every field offers a unique viewpoint into Agentic Studies, producing vital cross-domain research.
