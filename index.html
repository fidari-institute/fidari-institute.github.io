Research Pillars
Mission

Why Agentic Studies?

As AI systems become increasingly autonomous, we need to know why they do what they do, helping us anticipate their actions. Fidari's main goal is not to prevent certain outcomes, though, but rather to study AI as an organism to discover and investigate its behaviors.


View the Agentic Studies Whitepaper

What does Fidari do?

Agentic Studies is a new way of thinking. No longer are we treating AI agents as the core unit of study. Instead, through interdisciplinary approaches from behavioral science, political science, and game theory, we can study agents as entities with the capacity to act. Thus, we can study how they use that ability, resulting in societal structures that aren't just behavior. Every field has something to offer.
